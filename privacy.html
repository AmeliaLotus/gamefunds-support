Privacy policy

Your records stay on your phone.

GameFunds doesn't collect anything about you. This page explains what happens to your information, including the few times it leaves the app because you chose to send it somewhere.

The short version: GameFunds does not collect your data. There's no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. This policy covers the GameFunds app for iPhone and this website.

1. Your records stay on your device

Your funds, transactions, savings goals, wishlist, recurring expenses, photos and settings are stored on your phone. None of it is sent to me, and I have no way to see it. GameFunds doesn't connect to your bank, and it doesn't sync your records to the cloud or to other devices.

2. Backups and exports

You can export a backup or a CSV file whenever you like, and you decide where it goes. A backup can include your financial records and photos, so keep it somewhere you trust. Once you save a file to a cloud service, email it or share it with someone, they have a copy, and their own privacy policy applies to it.

If your iPhone backs itself up to iCloud or a computer, that backup may include GameFunds data too. Apple and your device settings control that, not GameFunds.

The app also keeps recovery copies on your phone. They're deleted with the app, so they don't replace a backup of your own. I don't keep a copy of your records anywhere, so I can't restore them for you, and no backup or restore is guaranteed to work.

3. Optional features

  • Photos: You choose photos with Apple's photo picker, so GameFunds only gets the photos you pick, not access to your whole library. They're stored on your phone with your records.
  • Face ID or Touch ID: The optional app lock asks iOS to check that it's you. GameFunds only gets a yes or no, never your face or fingerprint data. The lock covers the app screen; it doesn't encrypt your data or any files you export.
  • Reminders: Reminders are notifications scheduled on your phone. You can turn them off in the Settings app at any time.
  • Widgets: Widgets can show fund balances on your Home Screen, where anyone looking at your phone can see them. Keep that in mind when you choose what to show.

4. Purchases go through Apple

GameFunds Pro Lifetime is an optional one-time purchase. Apple handles the payment and tells the app whether you own Pro. I never see your card details. Apple's privacy policy covers the purchase itself. Restoring Pro brings back Pro, not your records.

5. If you email me

If you write to gamefunds.support@gmail.com, I get your email address, your message and anything you attach. I only use that to answer you and to look into the problem you report.

The support inbox is a Gmail account, so Google's privacy policy applies too, along with your own email provider's. Emails stay in the inbox unless you ask me to delete them, which I'm happy to do. Your email provider may keep its own copy.

Please don't send passwords, payment details, financial records or backup files, and cover up anything personal in screenshots.

6. This website

This site has no analytics, ads, forms, embedded content from other sites or cookies. Everything it shows, including fonts and images, comes from the site itself.

It's hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security. See GitHub's Pages information and GitHub's privacy statement. Links to other sites, and the email link, take you to services with their own privacy policies.

7. Your choices

What you put into GameFunds is yours to keep or delete. You can turn off reminders, remove widgets and change the app lock whenever you like.

Deleting the app deletes its data, including the recovery copies, so export a backup first if you want to keep anything. Files you exported or emailed elsewhere need to be deleted there. Your purchase history is managed by Apple.

Because your records only exist on your phone, I can't view, change or delete them for you.

8. Changes and questions

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top.

Questions or requests about privacy? Email gamefunds.support@gmail.com.
