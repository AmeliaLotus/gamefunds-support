Privacy, in plain language.

GameFunds keeps personal budgeting local. Here’s what that means for your records—and the services you choose to use.

No GameFunds account, app backend, analytics or advertising. Everyday budgeting works offline. This policy covers the GameFunds iOS app and its support website.

1. Your app data stays local

GameFunds stores your personal funds, ledger entries, savings goals, wishlist, recurring entries, selected photos and app preferences on your device. These records are not automatically sent to the developer. GameFunds does not connect to your bank.

There is no automatic GameFunds cloud sync. Household sharing is not available in the launch version.

2. Backups and exports are your choice

GameFunds provides manual exports and local recovery copies. On-device copies are stored locally and can be lost when the app is deleted or the device is lost, damaged or reset. They are not an independent, off-device backup.

When you export or share a file, you choose its destination through iOS. An exported backup may contain personal financial information and photos. If you save it to a third-party storage service, email it or share it with someone, that service or recipient receives the file. Their handling of it is governed by their own practices and policies.

Apple-managed device backups may also include eligible app data, depending on your settings. These are separate from GameFunds exports and are not automatic GameFunds cloud sync. No backup or successful restore is guaranteed.

Keep a separate backup in a location you trust. GameFunds does not maintain a server copy from which the developer can recover your records.

3. Optional device features

  • Photos: You select images with the iOS system picker. GameFunds uses the selected images locally; using this picker does not require broad access to your photo library.
  • Face ID or Touch ID: The optional screen lock uses iOS authentication. GameFunds receives the authentication result, not your face or fingerprint templates. The lock controls access to the app screen; it is not a promise of encryption for app data, backups or exports.
  • Reminders: Optional reminders use local iOS notifications. You control notification permission and visibility in iOS Settings.
  • Widgets: GameFunds makes the information needed for its widgets available locally to its widget extension. A widget can display financial information outside the app screen. Consider what is visible when choosing and placing widgets.

4. Purchases are handled by Apple

GameFunds Pro Lifetime is an optional one-time in-app purchase. Apple processes the payment and verifies purchases and restoration. GameFunds uses purchase and entitlement information to determine whether Pro is available. The developer does not receive your full payment-card details from this purchase.

Apple handles information associated with its services under Apple’s privacy policy. Purchase restoration restores eligible Pro access, not your locally stored financial records.

5. If you contact support

If you email gamefunds.support@gmail.com, the developer receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. This information is used to respond to your request and investigate the issue you report.

Support email is handled through Gmail and your chosen email provider, under their respective policies, including Google’s privacy policy. Email correspondence may remain in the support mailbox. You can contact the same address to request deletion of your correspondence; provider retention rules and applicable obligations may affect what can be removed.

Please do not send passwords, payment details, financial records or personal backup files. Remove personal information from screenshots before sending them.

6. This support website

The website’s pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising, forms, embedded third-party content or cookies set by GameFunds. Fonts and visual assets are served with the site.

When this site is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub processes website requests and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. This is separate from the app’s local financial records. See GitHub’s Pages information and GitHub’s privacy statement.

Following an external link or opening your email service takes you to a service governed by its own privacy practices.

7. Your choices and deletion

You control the records you enter, photos you select, exports you create, and optional features you enable. You can manage notification permissions in iOS Settings, remove widgets, and change the app’s lock settings.

Deleting the app removes its local app data, including local recovery copies. Export a backup first if you want to retain your records. Files you exported elsewhere and messages you sent remain separate: delete them with the relevant storage or email provider. Apple purchase records are managed by Apple.

The developer cannot view, edit or delete financial records stored only on your device.

8. Updates and questions

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to GameFunds or the services described here. The date at the top identifies the latest revision.

For privacy questions or requests, contact GameFunds at gamefunds.support@gmail.com.
