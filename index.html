Everyday use

What can I do with GameFunds?

Split your gaming money into funds (for example PC, Console and Handheld), log what you add and spend, and keep a wishlist of what you want to buy next. You can also save toward bigger purchases with goals, track subscriptions that renew each month, and add a widget to your Home Screen.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

You never need an account, and everything works offline. The only time you need the internet is to buy or restore Pro, because that goes through Apple.

Do my records sync between devices?

No. Your records stay on the phone you use GameFunds on. To move them, export a backup and import it on the other device (see Backups & recovery below).

Backups & recovery

How do I back up my records?

Open the menu, go to Backup & Recovery and save a backup to Files, iCloud Drive or wherever you like. Keep it somewhere outside the app, so it's still there if you lose or reset your phone. CSV exports are handy for spreadsheets, but only a GameFunds backup can be restored.

The app also keeps recovery copies on your phone. They're a safety net, not a real backup, because they're deleted along with the app. If you save a backup to a cloud service, that service's own terms and privacy policy apply to the file.

Can you recover my data if it's gone?

Only if you still have a backup. I never see your records, so there's no copy on my end. If you have an exported backup, or the app still has a recovery copy, use Backup & Recovery to restore it. Save your current records first, and read the confirmation before you tap restore.

One tip: don't delete and reinstall the app to fix a problem. That deletes your records too.

How do I move to a new phone?

Export a backup on your old phone and make sure you can open it on the new one (saving it to iCloud Drive makes this easy). Install GameFunds on the new phone, import the backup and check that everything is there before you wipe the old one. Restoring Pro doesn't bring your records over; only the backup does that.

GameFunds Pro

What does Pro Lifetime include?

GameFunds Pro Lifetime adds unlimited funds, every accent theme and custom colors for your fund icons. It's a one-time in-app purchase of US $4.99, with no subscription, and the purchase sheet shows the price for your App Store region before you confirm.

Free will always include 3 funds and the base features, including the wishlist, savings goals, recurring expenses and spending insights.

How do I restore Pro?

Sign in with the same Apple Account you bought it with, connect to the internet and tap Restore Purchases on the Pro screen. If Pro still doesn't show up, check your purchase history in the App Store, then email me with your app version and any error message you see. Never send your Apple Account password or payment details.

Privacy & troubleshooting

How do photos and the app lock work?

When you add a photo, you choose it with Apple's photo picker, so GameFunds only gets the photo you pick. It's stored on your phone with the rest of your records. The optional app lock uses Face ID or Touch ID through iOS, and GameFunds only learns whether it worked. The lock protects the app screen, not backup files you've exported. There's more detail in the privacy policy.

A reminder or widget isn't showing up. What can I check?

For reminders, check the schedule in GameFunds, then make sure notifications are allowed for GameFunds in the iPhone Settings app. Focus modes can hide them too. For widgets, open GameFunds once so it can refresh, then touch and hold the widget to check its settings. iOS decides when widgets update, so a change can take a little while to appear.

How do I report a bug or suggest something?

Use Help & Feedback in the app's menu, or email me. For a bug, tell me your app version, iOS version, what you expected and what happened instead. A screenshot helps a lot; just cover up anything personal first. Accessibility feedback is very welcome.
