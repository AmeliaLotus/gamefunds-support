Everyday use

What can I do with GameFunds?

Organize your allowance or budget into personal funds, record deposits and expenses in a ledger, and plan with savings goals and a prioritized wishlist. Recurring entries, selected photos, local reminders and Home Screen widgets help make it your own.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No account is needed. Everyday budgeting works offline, and GameFunds does not connect to your bank or run an app backend. Purchasing or restoring Pro uses Apple’s services and may require an internet connection. Sending support email and opening this website also use external services.

Can I share a fund with my household?

Household sharing is not available at launch. GameFunds is designed for your personal funds, with no automatic syncing of financial records between devices.

Backups & recovery

How do I keep a backup of my records?

Open Backup & Recovery in the app’s menu to create a manual backup and choose where to save it. Keep a copy somewhere you can access independently of the app and your device. CSV exports in Spending Insights are useful for reviewing records; use the app’s backup format for restoration.

On-device recovery copies are local. They can be lost if you delete the app or lose your device. If you choose a cloud drive or another provider for an export, that provider handles the saved file under its own terms and privacy policy.

Can support recover missing data?

GameFunds has no server copy of your financial records. If you still have an on-device recovery copy or an exported backup, use Backup & Recovery to review your restore options. Save your current records before restoring, and read the in-app confirmation carefully.

If the app and its local data are gone and you have no usable backup, support cannot retrieve those records. Avoid deleting and reinstalling the app as a first troubleshooting step.

How do I move to a new device?

Before changing devices, export a backup and make sure you can access the file from your new device. Install GameFunds and use Backup & Recovery to import the backup. Confirm your records are present before removing anything from the old device. Restoring a Pro purchase does not restore your financial records.

GameFunds Pro

What does Pro Lifetime include?

Free includes 3 active personal funds. GameFunds Pro Lifetime unlocks unlimited personal funds and additional accent colors with a one-time in-app purchase of US $4.99. There is no subscription. The purchase sheet shows the price for your App Store region before you confirm.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase, connect to the internet, and choose Restore Purchases in the app’s Pro settings. Apple verifies purchase eligibility. If Pro does not appear, check your Apple purchase history and contact support with the app version and any error message. Do not send payment details or your Apple Account password.

Privacy & troubleshooting

How do photos and the optional app lock work?

You choose photos through the iOS system picker. Selected photos are stored locally for use in GameFunds. The optional Face ID or Touch ID screen lock uses the system authentication prompt; GameFunds does not receive your biometric templates. A screen lock is not a backup and does not protect an exported file stored elsewhere. Read the privacy policy for details.

Why is a reminder or widget not showing?

For reminders, check the schedule in GameFunds and notification permission in iOS Settings. Focus modes and notification settings can affect what you see. For widgets, open GameFunds to check the current information, then check your widget configuration. iOS controls widget refresh timing, so changes may not appear immediately.

How do I report a problem or suggest a feature?

Use Help & Feedback in the app, or email the address below. For a problem, include your app version, iOS version, what you expected and what happened. A screenshot with personal information removed can help. Accessibility feedback is welcome, too.
